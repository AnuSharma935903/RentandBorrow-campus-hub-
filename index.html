How Rent & Borrow Works

Simple, safe, and student-verified peer sharing in 3 easy steps.

01

Find an item

Browse textbooks, graphing calculators, lab gear, and cameras listed by verified student peers right on your campus.

02

Request to rent

Select your borrow duration, review the daily credit rate and refundable security deposit, and send an instant request.

03

Borrow and return

Meet at an on-campus safe zone (library or student union), use the item for your coursework, and return safely to release deposit.
